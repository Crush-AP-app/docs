Crush AP is built to make difficult reasoning teachable. We show students how an expert approaches a problem, ask them to produce each important step, correct errors quickly, and return to the skill until it holds.

The goal is not to remove thinking. The goal is to remove avoidable guessing so students can spend their effort on physics.

## The learning loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Teach the new idea clearly">
    State or demonstrate the exact rule, distinction, or routine the student needs. Keep the amount of new information small.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the student respond">
    Students identify, calculate, explain, draw, or choose. A lesson is a sequence of productions, not a page of text with a Continue button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct the exact error">
    Name the mistake briefly, give the correct answer once, and repeat the task. The learner shows that the correction landed by responding again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return before the skill fades">
    Review expands from one day to longer intervals. Harder skills also refresh the foundations they use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A system, not a collection of tips

Our learning design connects three levels:

- **Instructional theory** defines what a clear presentation and a useful example sequence must do.
- **Lesson design** turns those principles into short screens, worked models, response prompts, and correction flows.
- **Product behavior** controls what unlocks next, when review returns, and what happens after a confirmed gap.

This documentation explains those connections. It is written for educators, school partners, families, and anyone who wants to understand the choices behind the app.

<Note>
  Crush AP continues to refine its lessons and product. These pages describe current design commitments, not a claim that every shipped screen is already perfect.
