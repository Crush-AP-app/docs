*Connecting Math Concepts* is one of Crush AP's main examples of Direct Instruction executed at high quality. We use it as a reference for instructional rhythm and sequence, then adapt those principles to self-paced AP Physics 1.

## The rhythm we borrow

The teacher scripts in *Connecting Math Concepts* move quickly between teaching and student production:

1. Name or demonstrate the new content.
2. Explain the rule only as much as needed.
3. Ask for a short response immediately.
4. Contrast the new content with a likely confusion.
5. Repeat related responses until they are firm.
6. Return to the prerequisite in later lessons before extending it.

Students are not asked to read a long explanation and report that they understand. Their responses create visible evidence throughout the lesson.

## How that becomes product behavior

| CMC principle | Crush AP implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Few sentences before a response | Single-purpose lesson screens with frequent questions |
| Teach a term or rule before testing it | Worked teaching lands the new content before independent use |
| Positive and negative examples | Mixed case sets and close contrasts |
| Repeat until firm | Correction gates and later fresh-variant retests |
| Bring back prerequisites before extending them | Retrieval prompts and a prerequisite-aware course sequence |
| Move from component steps to a complete routine | Guided setup followed by increasingly independent problems |

## What changes in a self-paced app

A scripted classroom teacher can hear a group response and repeat a series immediately. An app must represent that logic through screens, inputs, state, and scheduling.

Crush AP therefore separates three jobs:

- the **lesson sequence** decides what to present and when to request a response;
- the **correction flow** responds to a miss without adding a lecture;
- the **mastery and review system** decides when the skill should return.

This is an adaptation, not a digital reproduction of the CMC materials. The source informs the learning design. Crush AP authors its own physics explanations, examples, interfaces, and assessments.
