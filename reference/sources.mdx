Crush AP draws from a specific Direct Instruction tradition and an evolving body of product evidence. This page explains how those sources are used and what happens when they disagree.

## Authority order

1. **Direct human rulings and recorded correction history.** These capture current instructional judgment and resolved product evidence.
2. **Connecting Math Concepts and Engelmann source material.** These provide the trusted instructional ground layer.
3. **Current pedagogy documents and executing contracts.** These translate the higher-level sources into lesson and product rules.
4. **Shipped lessons.** These show how a rule has been implemented, but a shipped screen is not automatically correct.

When two sources conflict, the higher source wins. If the trusted sources do not resolve the conflict, the team requests a human ruling rather than silently choosing one.

## Primary references

### Rubric for Identifying Authentic Direct Instruction Programs

Siegfried Engelmann's 2006 rubric supplies adopted criteria from Category 1 on presentation, Category 2 on tasks, Category 4 on exercises, and Category 7 on organization of content. Crush AP adapts these criteria into its own presentation contract while treating the original rubric as the authority on intent. Categories 3, 5, and 6 remain deferred.

### Connecting Math Concepts

Teacher materials from SRA's *Connecting Math Concepts* provide concrete examples of instructional rhythm, discrimination teaching, component-to-routine sequencing, correction, mastery tests, remediation, and cumulative review.

Crush AP studies those patterns and authors original AP Physics instruction. It does not reproduce the source curriculum.

### Theory of Instruction: Principles and Applications

Engelmann and Carnine's *Theory of Instruction: Principles and Applications* provides a broader account of how examples, sequencing, and learner responses can be designed to support reliable generalization.

## How product evidence is used

The team also studies real interactions, lesson reviews, quality-check results, and correction pilots. Product evidence can refine how a principle is executed. It does not override the trusted instructional ground layer without an explicit ruling.

For example, a repair-first table correction was retained after product testing showed that revealing the completed table before repair weakened the interaction. That exception is recorded as a surface-specific ruling rather than generalized to every correction.

## How these pages stay current

Each public page is listed in a machine-readable source map. The map names the private files that may govern that page and whether human review is required.

The documentation automation follows four rules:

1. React only to mapped source changes.
2. Make the smallest accurate public update.
3. Open a pull request with the source paths and source commit.
4. Never merge a semantic change automatically.

<Note>
  Public documentation is a derived explanation. It is not a new source of pedagogy authority, and it is never fed back into the lesson system as law.
</Note>
